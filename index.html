About Us

The World Has Many Organizations helping the Needy People but None Is Targeting The Mentally Surviving Persons And Yet When Community Knows That The Person Has a History Of Mental illness. They Try So Hard To Remove A person From there Current Job, School or Education Program and Also From The Community. Future For All Needy Persons Is a Non Government Funded Program That Aims At bettering The Life Of Mentally Stable Persons, We as a team that is aiming at bridging the gap . Our Main Target is to build a Society that Supports The Stable Mental Person To Encourage On there Skills and Give chance For better Life.

Education Support

Health & Wellbeing

Shelter Construction.

Most Organizations build For Needy Persons But Few Of Them Help to Construct a good Shelter For A person with the History Of Mental illness. FANP does go to an extent to build for the mentally stable persons shelters so as to try and improve the standards of living in the community.

Feeding The Needy.

Feed A Needy Person Today, You have Feed a Whole Nation Tomorrow. Currently The State Of Living is getting Complex, most Families Survive on One Meal Yet Some Needy Persons Are Vulnerable, This Puts the State Of Their life At risk Of Death. FANP takes this opportunity to look and find those needy person since most families ignore a person with mental illness believing they should eat from the bins. We Take This Chance To Feed The mentally Stable Persons and Their Families.

Job Creation

Today We are here for Something Bigger than Help, We are Here For Hope. Feeding a person For feeds them For one Day, But giving a Person a Job ,Skill and a Chance to earn that feeds them For a life time. FANP takes a extra step to create job opportunities for the mentality stable persons since most employees get to understand that a person has history of mental illness they don't hire them forget that disability is not inablility.We Also Try to Give career Advice For The mentally Stable Persons So as they can become beneficial to the families and Community at large Since Most Mentally Stables Person Are fathers and Mothers With Children Aiming at them to provide basic needs.

Youth Empowerment

The main Cause Of Youth Running Mad is stress because most of them finish school and no jobs for what they studied For . FANP goes ahead to create and install skills into the youths so as to be able to buy them selves whatever they need rather ending up stealing and risking early death. When We Skill The Youth With Income Generating Activities We Ready the number of Mental illness,Unwated marriages Among The Girl Child,Sexually transmitted Diseases because Of Money,Sucide rate Also Drops.

Health And Counselling

Communities Separate a person who has a history of mental illness even if the person has recovered. FANP Goes A head to Counsel the Mentally Stable Person and the Community To Since Mental illness is not Contagious. We also Encourage the Mentaly Stable Persons To Continue With medication.

Religious Support
