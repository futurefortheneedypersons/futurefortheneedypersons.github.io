About Us

The World Has Many Organizations helping the Needy People but None Is Targeting The Mentally Surviving Persons And Yet When Community Knows That The Person Has a History Of Mental illness, They Try So Hard To Remove A person From there Current Job, School or Education Program and Also From The Community. Future For All Needy Persons Is a Non Government Funded Program That Aims At bettering The Life Of Mentally Stable Persons, We as a team is aiming at bridging the gap by restoring full functionality and productivity of the needy stable mental persons hence fighting stigma. Our Main Target is to build a Society that Supports The Stable Mental Person To Encourage On there Skills and Give chance For better Life. OUR MISSION Fighting Stigma Among Mentally Stable Persons through Creating Skills, Job Creation, Counselling And Community Sensitizations. OUR VISION Restore A Healthy, Full Functionality and Productivity in A needy Stable Mental Persons. BRIEF BACKGROUND Mental illness Is a chronic illness because majority of affected persons are born normal then sickness occurs due to many underlying factors, Due To Family And Community Perception that the mentally ill person's are seen as a burden hence leading to Stigma And Discrimition Of The Stable Mental Persons. Future For All Needy Persons was created with the Aim To bridge the gap for All Stable Mental Persons Thus Fighting Stigma In All Ways Possible So As For A Better Community.
